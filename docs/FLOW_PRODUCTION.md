## Prompts que produziram os clipes aprovados

### Amazonita

> Create an 8-second silent 16:9 cinematic material study from the supplied real Amazonita stone photograph. Preserve the exact mineral veins, turquoise-green coloration and texture. Begin as an extreme macro close-up of this same stone surface and perform only a very slow controlled lateral dolly across it, revealing slightly more of the slab. Large diffused studio softbox, restrained directional highlights, deep natural shadows, realistic polished reflections, precise focus. Sophisticated, tactile, editorial and quiet. No people, typography, captions, logos, additional objects, invented patterns, dramatic camera moves or fantasy minerals. Keep the source photograph clearly recognizable throughout.

### Rooftop Arquitetura

> Create an 8-second silent 16:9 editorial film of the supplied Rooftop Arquitetura identity artwork. Treat the uploaded image as a finished, locked design: preserve the exact Rooftop wordmark, vertical symbol, letters, colors, proportions and layout in every frame. The artwork is printed on a premium matte presentation board in a dark, minimal architectural studio. Keep the board nearly front-facing and camera movement extremely slow and subtle, with a restrained lateral dolly of only a few centimeters. Let soft controlled light reveal the texture of the board. Do not redraw, animate, morph, replace or invent any graphic, logo, letter or text. No people, buildings, property footage, extra objects, particles or flashy effects. Quiet, precise, photorealistic and premium.

### Simetria Estética

> Create an 8-second silent 16:9 cinematic identity study using the supplied Simetria Estética artwork as a locked, exact reference. Keep the supplied Simetria symbol, name, typography, olive-white color, matte green background and modular bottom pattern unchanged in every frame; do not redraw, morph or invent any graphic or lettering. Present it as a premium printed identity board with very subtle tactile paper texture and soft natural studio light. Camera is nearly front-facing with a very slow lateral dolly and delicate shadow motion. Refined, calm, balanced, precise and editorial. No models, doctors, treatments, beauty imagery, extra brand assets, slogans, random props, particles or dramatic effects. This is a conceptual visual identity presentation, not footage of a real location.
